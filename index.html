Our Story

Where it began

Our story began at Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), where we shared more than lecture halls and campus life.

We attended the same church, Higher Ground Baptist Church, and belonged to the Faculty of Engineering. Olamide studied Food Engineering, while Toluwani studied Computer Engineering and was a year ahead.

At church, Toluwani served on the Technical Team, while Olamide was part of the Media Department. It was there that she first caught his attention.

But our friendship took a new turn one day when Olamide accompanied Toluwani to the school library to complete his SIWES report. What began as a simple visit grew into conversations, friendship and, eventually, something more.

In April 2020, we officially began our relationship.

Since then, we have shared years of growth, school experiences, graduations, National Youth Service and many moments that have shaped our lives together.

Through it all, we have found love in friendship, strength in each other and joy in growing together.

Now, after more than six years together, we look forward to the next chapter.

And so, our forever begins.

Getting There

Finding the venue

Lead City University International Conference Centre, Ibadan.

From Iwo Road

  1. Take a bus, cab or bike heading towards Toll Gate.
  2. Tell the driver you are going to Lead City University, not just Toll Gate.
  3. Alight at Lead City University Main Gate.
  4. Once inside the university, ask security personnel for directions to the Conference Centre.

From Challenge

  1. Take a bus, cab or bike heading towards Toll Gate.
  2. Confirm that the vehicle passes through Lead City University.
  3. Alight at Lead City University Main Gate.
  4. Ask security personnel for directions to the Conference Centre.

From Toll Gate

  1. Take a bike or cab to Lead City University, off Oba Otudeko Avenue.
  2. Ask to be dropped at the university's Main Gate.
  3. Once inside, follow directions to the Conference Centre.

Important: Please tell drivers that your destination is Lead City University, not Toll Gate generally. This will help avoid being dropped at the wrong location.
